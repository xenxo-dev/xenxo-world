We don't ask for email, phone or password. Your passkey stays on your device: we only receive its public key, which can't be used to impersonate you. Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose it, your browser goes to Google and Google returns only an identifier of your account (no email or name); we store its fingerprint (hash), not the identifier. Google, as controller of its own service, will know you sign in to xenxo.io (see its privacy policy). You can remove Google from your account at any time in Account. We use no tracking or advertising cookies and build no profiles. Preferences (language, graphics quality, ambience, controls, chosen ready-made character, session) are stored only in your browser. So the site opens fast and can be installed as an app, your browser also keeps a copy of its files (code, textures, icons; no personal data), which you can clear from your browser settings. Photos from photo mode are created on your device and are not sent anywhere. Your character's portrait (head and shoulders, 256 px) is also made in your browser whenever you change character; that one we do keep, as part of your content, for the games you give permission to.

3. Where it's stored and who sees it

Account, project and world data are stored in the European Union on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Durable Objects and R2 with EU jurisdiction), acting as processor. Cloudflare is a US-based company; any transfers are covered by its standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your display name, player number, the character you're wearing and your projects are public to visitors of xenxo.io. The other characters in your wardrobe are only visible to you. We don't sell data or share it with third parties, except with the games you allow through “Sign in with xenxo” (below).

The list of GitHub repositories is fetched by our server: your browser does not connect to GitHub. External links published by other people lead to pages we don't control.

Your player card. It's private unless you make it public in Account → Your card. If you do, anyone with its link (xenxo.io/p/your-number) sees your number, display name, the character you're wearing, your sign-up date and your published projects. We only store whether it's on or off; turning it off or deleting your account hides it straight away. The card image is created in your browser: we don't keep it.

“Sign in with xenxo” in other people's games. If you sign in to a game with your xenxo account, you first see which game it is, who made it, which website you return to and what it asks for. Only if you press “Allow” do we send it, signed by xenxo: an identifier for you that only works in that game and, depending on what it asks, your display name, your player number (with it, different games can tell you're the same person) and the link to your character and its portrait. The terms require game makers to use your character and portrait only to represent you in the session: they may not store, sell or use them for anything else. We keep which permission you gave to which game and when it was last used, so we don't ask again and you can revoke it in Account → Connected games. What the game does with that data is the responsibility of whoever makes it, not xenxo. If you register a game, we keep its name and redirect addresses.

Other people's games inside xenxo. Before opening a game you see who makes it, where it's hosted, its age rating, whether it has ads or purchases and whether xenxo has reviewed it. If you open it, your browser connects to that game's website (as when visiting any website: it sees your IP address and what your browser sends), and it opens isolated, with no access to your session or anything stored by xenxo. xenxo only tells it your language. Nothing else, except what you allow with “Sign in with xenxo”. What the game does is governed by its own privacy policy, linked in the notice.

Publishing a game. If you register your game's card (its xenxo.json), our server reads it from your website and keeps a copy: name, description, age rating, devices, ads or purchases, author, contact and privacy page. That card is public.

Moderation. What's published is reviewed before it shows in the catalogue, and moderation can hide it. For each decision we keep what was decided, when, who moderated and the reason (required by the Digital Services Act); the author sees the reason for a rejection or hiding. If you moderate, we keep since when (it is included in your data export) and the moderation team sees your number and name; adding or removing you goes in the log. If you delete your account and you moderated, your decisions are kept without your name.

Community districts. Until moderation approves it, only you and moderation can see your district. Once approved, its name, description, cover, content and your name and player number as its author are public; the contact you put in the card is only seen by moderation. People enter your district with their xenxo account: you receive none of their data.

Scoreboards. If you complete a hunt, set a race time or score points on a tile board, your name, your player number and that time (or the date, or the day’s points) may appear on that district’s scoreboard, visible to anyone passing by. Guests play without a scoreboard.

Jams. Games entered into a jam are public, and when voting ends their average stars and number of votes are published. Who voted what is never published: each person only sees their own votes. To vote, the account must predate the voting or be more than a day old (to prevent accounts made for the occasion). If moderation removes a game from a jam, its author sees the reason and the decision is logged.

4. How long

  • Visitor sessions: while you're connected.
  • Account and content: until you delete your account or the content.
  • Files you upload that nothing uses (a character you neither keep in your wardrobe nor wear, a cover or monument with no project): deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Permissions given to games (“Sign in with xenxo”): until you revoke them or delete your account. The codes and tokens games receive expire within an hour at most.
  • Your community district: until you delete it or your account; its files are then deleted like anything else nothing uses.
  • Activity progress and times: while your account exists; deleting it also removes them from the scoreboards.
  • Jam entries and votes: while the jam, the project or your account exist; deleting your account deletes your votes and entries (and the votes your games received).
  • Chat messages: 7 days. If someone reports a message, a copy is kept with the report.
  • A game's card: while the project is published. Moderation decision log: 5 years, to be able to answer complaints.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after they are resolved, unless the law requires longer.

5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability. Under "Account" on the site itself you can, without writing to us:

  • Download your data as a JSON file (access and portability).
  • Change your name (rectification).
  • Delete your account: your account, passkeys, Google link, projects, their portals and the models and covers only you uploaded are deleted immediately (erasure).

For anything else, write to contact@xenxo.io. If you think we haven't handled your rights properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local authority.

6. Minors

You must be 14 or older to create an account, the age from which people can consent to data processing in Spain (Art. 7 LOPDGDD). Anyone can visit the plaza without an account and without giving any data.

7. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll ask you to accept the new version next time you sign in.
