We don't ask for email, phone or password. Your passkey stays on your device: we only receive its public key, which can't be used to impersonate you. We use no tracking or advertising cookies and build no profiles. Preferences (language, graphics quality, session) are stored only in your browser.

3. Where it's stored and who sees it

Account, project and world data are stored in the European Union on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Durable Objects and R2 with EU jurisdiction), acting as processor. Cloudflare is a US-based company; any transfers are covered by its standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your display name, character and projects are public to visitors of xenxo.io. We don't sell or share data with third parties.

The list of GitHub repositories is fetched by our server: your browser does not connect to GitHub. External links published by other people lead to pages we don't control.

4. How long

  • Visitor sessions: while you're connected.
  • Account and content: until you delete your account or the content.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after they are resolved, unless the law requires longer.

5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability. Under "Customize" on the site itself you can, without writing to us:

  • Download your data as a JSON file (access and portability).
  • Change your name (rectification).
  • Delete your account: your account, passkey, projects, their stands and the models only you uploaded are deleted immediately (erasure).

For anything else, write to [contact email]. If you think we haven't handled your rights properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local authority.

6. Minors

You must be 14 or older to create an account, the age from which people can consent to data processing in Spain (Art. 7 LOPDGDD). Anyone can visit the plaza without an account and without giving any data.

7. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll ask you to accept the new version next time you sign in.
